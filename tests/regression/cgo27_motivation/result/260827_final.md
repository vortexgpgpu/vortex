### Findings

- m14 winner counts over 32 shape x epilogue cells: c1=2, c2=8, c3=1,
  c4=12, c6=4, c8=4, c10=1. c12/c14/c16 win no cell.
- m15 winner counts: c1=3, c2=9, c3=7, c4=7, c6=4, c8=2.
  c10/c12/c14/c16 win no cell. No single width is universally best.
- Adding c6/c10/c12/c14/c16 changes nine optima relative to the low-width
  sweep: five m14 cells and four m15 cells. c6 accounts for seven changes.
- m14: the best tested point improves on c15 in all 32 cells; median 6.9%,
  range 0.6% to 49.3%.
- m15: the best tested point improves on c15 in all 32 cells; median 15.5%,
  range 1.3% to 70.2%.
- All 64 requested-c16 results exactly equal the corresponding c15 result,
  confirming the expected clamp to 15 effective consumer warps.
- Against the non-pipelined placement with the same engine, tuned m14 beats m7 in 13/32 cells and tuned m15 beats m8 in 14/32 cells. These wins occur mainly at larger cubic/attention shapes with GELU or Residual, where c15 had excessive core-to-engine L2 contention.
